## Angles
- A001: Treat the 20% weekly turnover cap as a scarce budget to be spent, not a limit to be clipped against: each day, allocate a fixed slice of the budget to the single trade with the highest marginal benefit per unit turnover (expected log-growth gain plus risk-contribution reduction), so the constraints shape the signal into a priority queue instead of fighting it.
- A002: Make the correlation structure itself the signal: use only the rolling average pairwise correlation and the effective number of independent bets (eigenvalue concentration of the 10-asset covariance from daily closes) to scale total risk down when diversification is collapsing, on the reasoning that the 20% drawdown bound is broken by correlated sell-offs (2008, 2020, 2022) and not by any single asset's trend.
- A003: Design backward from the de-risking speed limit: the 20% weekly turnover cap is also a cap on how fast risk can be cut, so treat the drawdown bound as a race between a correlated sell-off and a slew-rate-limited exit. Size the risk budget on each day from the running drawdown cushion minus the worst plausible loss the current book can suffer during the number of days it would take to de-risk at the allowed daily turnover (estimated from trailing close-to-close covariance of the held weights), so the cushion explicitly prices the lag that the turnover rule creates, rather than assuming instant stop-outs.
- A004: Frame allocation as online learning on the probability simplex: each trading day take one bounded mirror-descent step (e.g. exponentiated-gradient on log-growth with an entropy or risk-contribution-equalising regulariser) whose step size is set so the L1 move per day is at most a fixed fraction of the weekly cap; daily trading and the turnover cap then fall out of the step-size choice, the entropy term enforces diversification at every date, and regret bounds against the best constant-rebalanced mix give a principled, parameter-light growth argument instead of a fitted signal.
- A005: Constraint-first architecture: treat the 3-days-per-week and 20% turnover limits as the structural skeleton rather than a post-hoc clip. Split capital into overlapping tranches, each re-targeting the full signal on a fixed, different weekday, so trades occur every day and each tranche's move is bounded by construction; then design the signal (what to forecast, at what horizon) around what a slow, staggered book can actually harvest.
- A006: Stress-state-first design: make the primary object a daily estimate of correlated-stress state from closing prices alone (for example the absorption ratio, i.e. the share of cross-asset variance carried by the top eigenvector of a rolling correlation matrix, plus realised versus expected diversification benefit), and let the strategy be a smooth function of that state. Risk-parity-style weights are the baseline; the drawdown bound is argued from how quickly the state variable rises before or during 2008, 2020 and 2022 rather than from asset-level trend signals.
- A007: Make the constraints the return engine rather than obstacles: rebalance every trading day by a fixed fraction of the gap between current weights and a risk-budgeted target (exponential partial adjustment). Frequent trading then harvests the rebalancing premium among low-correlation, mean-reverting relative prices (volatility pumping, 'Shannon's demon'), and the adjustment fraction is set analytically so that the worst-case weekly turnover stays at or below 20% whatever the gap. The signal changes where the target sits, never how fast the portfolio is allowed to chase it.
- A008: Treat the 20% drawdown limit as a consumable budget that is part of the portfolio's state: risky exposure is a function of the remaining headroom to a floor that ratchets up with the high-water mark. That is paired with a correlation-regime detector computed from closing prices (the average pairwise 60-day correlation, or the share of variance in the first eigenvalue) that shrinks the headroom multiplier before diversification fails in a correlated sell-off. The worst-case drawdown can then be argued as a bound: headroom multiplied by an assumed gap-down size, plus the lag imposed by the turnover cap, rather than estimated from history.

## Domains
- D001: Network congestion control (TCP AIMD and rate-limited senders): additive increase and multiplicative decrease of a sending window under a shared-capacity constraint, with backoff triggered by loss signals; analogous to slowly adding risk per asset, cutting it sharply on stress, and never exceeding a per-day transfer rate.
- D002: Ecological early-warning signals of tipping points (critical slowing down): rising variance and lag-1 autocorrelation in a system's state variable precede regime collapse; analogous to detecting fragility in cross-asset return series from daily closes before a correlated crash, then shifting weights toward the assets that recover fastest.
- D003: Fisheries harvest control rules: a 'hockey-stick' rule cuts fishing mortality linearly once estimated biomass falls below a precautionary reference point, while TAC stability clauses cap year-to-year catch changes (often +/-15%) and include an override when stock falls below a critical limit; transfers as a drawdown-triggered risk-scaling rule with a rate-of-change cap and a pre-registered emergency exception budget.
- D004: Athlete load management in sports science: the acute:chronic workload ratio (e.g. 7-day vs 28-day load) keeps training load within a 'sweet spot' band and limits week-on-week load increases to roughly 10% to avoid injury spikes; transfers as ratcheting exposure asymmetrically (slow ramp-up, fast cut-down) using short-vs-long realised volatility ratios per asset class.
- D005: Evolutionary biology of bet-hedging and phenotypic switching in microbes: populations survive fluctuating environments by maintaining a diversified mix of phenotypes with switching rates tuned to environmental autocorrelation and to the noisiness of the sensed cue, which is a biological analogue of Kelly growth under uncertain regime signals; the transferable mechanism is a switching rate set by cue reliability and environmental persistence.
- D006: Power-grid frequency regulation: droop control with slew-rate-limited primary response plus spinning reserve held back as headroom. Generators respond proportionally to a measured deviation, ramp limits prevent oscillation, and reserve is pre-positioned so a sudden loss of supply does not collapse the system; the transferable mechanism is a proportional deviation-to-action law with a hard ramp cap and a deliberately held cash reserve.
- D007: Reservoir operation under drought 'hedging rules': operators ration releases progressively as storage falls through rule-curve zones instead of releasing at full demand until empty, and they obey limits on how fast releases may ramp and on minimum flows each day. This maps to drawdown-zone de-risking under a turnover cap that limits the rate of change.
- D008: Closed-loop insulin delivery (the artificial pancreas): a controller doses in small increments every few minutes under hard limits on the delivery rate, and it tracks 'insulin on board' (committed action that has not yet taken effect) so that it does not overshoot into hypoglycaemia. This maps to rate-limited daily trading toward a target with an explicit account of adjustments still pending under the turnover budget.
